## 2. Empirical vs. Synthetic Status Disclosure

- **Synthetic Demo Mode (`configs/demo.yaml`, `data_mode="synthetic_demo"`, badge `DEMO / SYNTHETIC`):**
  - **Status:** Fully executed, verified, and exported to `data/demo/`, `artifacts/demo/`, and [`reports/demo/results_summary.md`](../reports/demo/results_summary.md).
  - **Key Result (`demo_v1`, 8 held-out test origins):** `dfl_pto` (`JointMultiSKUMarginalAllocationModel` with shared store capacity $C_s$) achieved the lowest mean out-of-sample cost (**51.32 MU**, fill rate **85.4%**, mean oracle regret **27.59 MU**, realized $\text{CVaR}_{0.90} = \text{109.80 MU}$), outperforming `supervised_pto` (**51.37 MU**, fill rate **85.6%**), `proportional_allocation` (**52.16 MU**, fill rate **85.2%**), and `equal_allocation` (**53.13 MU**, fill rate **84.5%**). Validation DRO tuning (`split='val'`) selected $\alpha^*=0.90, \text{risk\_weight}^*=0.25$, under which `dfl_dro` (**55.04 MU**, $\text{CVaR}_{0.90} = \text{124.65 MU}$) outperformed `supervised_dro` (**56.91 MU**, $\text{CVaR}_{0.90} = \text{132.05 MU}$). Under multi-period rolling inventory carryover (`L=0` weeks), `supervised_dro` (**44.32 MU**), `dfl_dro` (**44.79 MU**), and `dfl_pto` (**49.60 MU**, `94.1%` fill rate) outperformed `equal_allocation` (**50.80 MU**), `supervised_pto` (**52.77 MU**), and `proportional_allocation` (**54.43 MU**).
- **Walmart M5 Regular-Demand Mode (`configs/m5.yaml`, `data_mode="m5_sales_proxy"`, `demand_regime="regular"`, badge `M5 sales proxy + simulated inventory`):**
  - **Status:** **COMPLETED & VERIFIED.** Ingested `data/raw/m5/` (`5` stores $\times$ `10` regular-selling `HOUSEHOLD` SKUs with training `ADI <= 1.25` $\times$ `120` weeks = `6,000` weekly rows; `100` train, `8` val, `12` test origins), verified zero temporal leakage (`audit_leakage.py` `"status": "PASS"`), trained `supervised` and joint multi-SKU `dfl` (`SPOPlus`) models, and executed the 7-policy backtest (`artifacts/m5/`, [`reports/m5/results_summary.md`](../reports/m5/results_summary.md), [`reports/results_summary.md`](../reports/results_summary.md)).
  - **Key Result (`m5_v1`, 12 held-out test origins):**
    - **Single-Period Out-of-Sample:** `dfl_pto` (**1312.70 MU**, fill rate **73.6%**, realized $\text{CVaR}_{0.90}$ **1686.33 MU**) and `supervised_dro` (**1315.14 MU**, fill rate **73.6%**, realized $\text{CVaR}_{0.90}$ **1677.40 MU**) both outperformed `supervised_pto` (**1322.76 MU**, fill rate **73.5%**, realized $\text{CVaR}_{0.90}$ **1708.12 MU**) and reduced cost by **-8.4% (-119.64 MU)** and **-8.2% (-117.20 MU)** vs `equal_allocation` (**1432.35 MU**). On steady single-period staples, trailing 8-week `proportional_allocation` achieved **1298.91 MU** mean cost, while **both `supervised_dro` (`1677.40 MU`), `dfl_pto` (`1686.33 MU`), and `dfl_dro` (`1689.26 MU`) beat `proportional_allocation` (`1711.69 MU`) on realized worst-tail risk ($\text{CVaR}_{0.90}$)**.
    - **Independent Training-Seed Stability (`[42, 101, 202]`, `P1.2`):** Across 3 independent neural network initializations + scenario seeds, `dfl_pto` (**1305.74 ± 6.05 MU**, `73.8%` fill rate) and `dfl_dro` (**1320.57 ± 6.98 MU**, `73.4%` fill rate) both outperformed `supervised_pto` (**1322.71 ± 4.41 MU**) and `supervised_dro` (**1324.02 ± 12.67 MU**).
    - **Multi-Period Rolling Inventory Carryover & Lead Time (`L=0` and `L=1`, `P1.1`):** With the exact multi-period horizon MILP oracle (`solve_multiperiod_carryover_oracle`) and strict mass conservation (`is_conserved = True`), rolling oracle regret is strictly nonnegative (`>= 0`):
      - At `L=0` (Immediate Replenishment): **`dfl_dro` achieved #1 lowest rolling mean cost (`1230.94 MU`, `76.5%` fill rate, regret `242.18 MU` vs oracle `988.77 MU`)**, followed closely by **`supervised_dro` #2 (`1233.16 MU`)** and **`dfl_pto` #3 (`1459.18 MU`)**, all substantially beating `supervised_pto` (`1526.37 MU`), `proportional_allocation` (`1620.85 MU`), and `equal_allocation` (`2080.03 MU`).
      - At `L=1` (1-Week Replenishment Lead Time, zero-leakage $\mathcal{F}_t$ feature projection): **`dfl_dro` achieved #1 lowest rolling mean cost (`1526.90 MU`, `70.2%` fill rate, regret `530.40 MU` vs oracle `996.49 MU`)**, followed by **`supervised_dro` #2 (`1565.34 MU`)** and **`dfl_pto` #3 (`1566.20 MU`)**, all beating `supervised_pto` (`1628.22 MU`), `proportional_allocation` (`1880.69 MU`), and `equal_allocation` (`2076.93 MU`).
