# Interview presentation guide

Use the corrected seven-slide deck `presentation_slides.pptx`, its local HTML/PDF fallback, and `PRESENTATION_SPEAKER_NOTES.md`. All numeric content is generated by `scripts/generate_presentation_deck.py` from the v2 summaries; its source hashes are saved in `reports/release/presentation_content.json`.

The prepared talk lasts approximately nine minutes: business decision (65 s), data/assumptions (75 s), methods (90 s), evaluation (80 s), findings (90 s), live decision example (70 s), limitations/pilot (70 s).

Lead with scarce-stock allocation, not advanced-model terminology. Define MU, sales proxy, PTO, DFL, oracle and CVaR briefly. Show the strongest simple baseline. Discuss any advanced-method loss or interval crossing zero without defensiveness. Carryover and sensitivity details belong in the appendix reports unless essential to a question.

Follow `OFFLINE_DEMO_GUIDE.md` for the 60-second local demonstration. Prepare the static fallback before entering the interview. Do a personal timed rehearsal on the actual laptop; software verification cannot certify delivery confidence.

Suggested answers:

- **Why not just use proportional allocation?** It is a required competitive baseline. If it wins under the relevant objective and evidence, keep it. The experiment tests when added complexity helps.
- **Is this genuine DFL?** Yes: a fixed-feasible PyEPO SPO+ optimization layer contributes measured gradients and parameter updates. A forecasting model followed by a solver alone would not qualify.
- **What does DRO protect against?** Scenario probability reweighting under an explicit bounded ambiguity set equivalent to CVaR. It is conditional protection within modeled scenarios, not a guarantee about all future demand.
- **Are these actual savings?** No. Historical M5 sales are demand proxies and economics are synthetic. A prospective pilot with real inventory data is needed.
- **Why only twelve weeks?** This is a bounded CPU research case study. Short time samples limit uncertainty and tail estimates; v2 is also retrospective after v1 inspection.
- **Can it handle the entire retailer network?** That scale has not been demonstrated. Real case packs, volumes, transport and latency constraints require separate modeling and benchmarking.
- **What would you do next?** Validate stock/cost/lead-time records, define the business endpoint and incumbent policy, then conduct a monitored planner-in-the-loop pilot on untouched data.
